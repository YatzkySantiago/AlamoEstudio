# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Businesses in Patagonia, Argentina, that need a website to bring in clients. They buy in Argentine pesos and are used to doing business over WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Álamo Estudio designs and builds landing pages and custom web apps for those businesses. The site exists to start a WhatsApp conversation with a prospective client. Success is a qualified person writing on WhatsApp.

## Positioning

A studio from Patagonia, working for businesses in Patagonia, that publishes its starting price.

## Operating Context

The exact quote is agreed in a WhatsApp conversation, based on project complexity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: landing pages and custom apps. Basic SEO and fast loading are part of every landing page, not a separate service.
- Google Ads is no longer offered. It must not appear anywhere: site copy, metadata, sitemap or anything else.
- Landing page price is published as "desde $80.000" (ARS). The upper reference of $275.000 is internal and is not published, so the studio can charge by complexity.
- Every landing page requires a monthly plan of $25.000 (ARS), which includes hosting, domain management ("gestión del dominio") and up to 2 modifications per month. Whether the domain registration itself is paid by the client is not stated on the site.
- Custom apps have no published price. Clients ask for a quote.
- WhatsApp is the main action. The site has no contact form; email is shown as plain contact information.
- Stack: React + Vite, already in place.
- Language: Spanish for Argentina, using voseo.

Undecided or unconfirmed:

- What a landing page includes at the starting price.
- Delivery times. The old copy said 2 to 6 weeks and a reply within 24 hs; neither has been confirmed.
- Whether the prices include IVA, and how often they are updated.
